How it is counted: 7.65% is the employer's share of Social Security and Medicare. State unemployment tax and workers' compensation come on top and vary by state, so add them to that box if you know yours. The benefits, software, equipment, days off and project-time figures are examples: replace them. A working year is 2,080 paid hours. Recruiting cost and the weeks a new hire takes to learn your standards are not included.

Where these numbers come from

  • Source: public job ads on Indeed for US building design production roles, pulled on Nov 12, 2026. 545 ads, 329 with stated pay.
  • What "pay" means: the middle of the range each ad states. Hourly rates are converted at 2,080 hours a year. This is what firms advertise, which is not always what they end up paying.
  • Who is included: firms of every size, not only small ones. Ads for factory, product, utility and civil drafting were removed, as were interns, managers and licensed engineers.
  • What it is not: a salary survey. No firm was asked anything. Use it as a view of the market on one day, next to your own judgment.
